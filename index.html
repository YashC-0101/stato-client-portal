C1 Lead Centre — feasibility & build plan TECHNICAL

Verdict: buildable — with three changes to the original plan. We went through Aircall's and Meta's documentation line by line instead of taking the plan at face value, and two assumptions in it work differently than expected. (1) Do not port the WhatsApp number. WhatsApp is tied to the number, not the SIM — moving 07440 517077 into Aircall gains nothing and risks the one thing that can't fail: the number is printed inside the QR code on the van, so if re-verification ever lands on a VoIP line and fails, that's a re-wrap, not a settings change. Meta's Coexistence route brings it in on its existing SIM, chat history intact, handset still answering. (2) A divert carries calls, not WhatsApp — the two are separate jobs, so WhatsApp (hours) doesn't wait on the port. (3) Six weeks, not 21 days — Aircall's own docs for UK ports — but the divert makes recording live in week one, so the wait costs nothing. Plus the three-asset ledger, what Coexistence costs you in features (7 constraints), the phase-by-phase sequence, running costs with the 1 October 2026 Meta pricing change nobody's mentioning, an 8-row risk ledger and the compliance points on call recording.
