† Why the Google figure proves the point

Querying Catchr for the Google PCP Claim account returns the same spend three times (once per OAuth reconnect). A naïve sum reads £5,646 — exactly the wrong number flagged earlier. Stato's de-duplication collapses the duplicates and returns the real £1,882, matching Catchr. The numbers are right because the platform understands how Catchr's data behaves.

Note on live figures: these are verified live and reconcile to source; they naturally move through the day as new leads deliver and ad spend accrues (Catchr syncs hourly). The point isn't a frozen number — it's that whenever you open Stato, what it shows matches what LeadByte / Catchr / Xero report.
