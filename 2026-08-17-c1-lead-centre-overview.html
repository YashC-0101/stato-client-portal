Where things stand

Two numbers, two places, no shared memory

Leads arrive on a WhatsApp number sitting in a van on a Lycamobile SIM, and on an office line. Neither knows about the other, and neither leaves a record anyone else can pick up.

Today

Whoever holds the phone holds the lead

  • Photos of a garage sit on one handset. Nobody else can quote from them.
  • A missed call at 07:14 leaves no trace and no follow-up.
  • No way to tell whether the van, Google or Meta produced a job.
  • If the phone breaks, the enquiry history goes with it.

After

The business holds the lead

  • Photos, calls and emails on one record, priced by anyone on the team.
  • Every missed call becomes a task with a timer on it.
  • Cost per lead by source, so ad spend has an answer.
  • Nothing lives on a handset. The number never has to change.

The constraint

The van has the number printed on it

Not in figures — inside the QR code. That is why the mobile number is treated as fixed for the life of the vehicle wrap.

Sequence

Wins first, irreversible steps last

Nothing risky happens in week one, and nothing is cancelled with any provider until a port has completed.

  • Days 1–3 · Phase 0

    Establish the facts

    Scan the van QR. Pull the Lycamobile account details. Confirm who holds the 0330 and whether it is the main billing number.

  • Week 1 · Phase 1

    Calls recorded and logged

    Aircall live with a divert from the office line. Recording, routing and voicemail working before any port is submitted.

  • Weeks 1–2 · Phase 2

    WhatsApp into the lead centre

    Coexistence onboarding on the mobile. This is the phase that stops leads being lost, and it is the fastest one.

  • Weeks 2–6 · Phase 3

    Port the office line

    Submitted with the divert still in place, so the cutover is invisible to callers.

  • Weeks 3–6 · Phase 4

    Email, forms, ads and the pipeline

    Photo-led quoting, response-time alerts, and cost per lead by source.

  • Later · Phase 5

    Automation on clean data

    Call summaries, postcode and volume extraction, out-of-hours replies.
