Overview

What went live

Both integrations run on the backend (the swift-server service on Railway). The website only forwards /api/* calls to it, so all mail and messages are sent server-side — keys never touch the browser. Turning each on was a matter of adding the provider credentials to the backend; the sending code was already built.

  • Email — provider Resend. The domain swiftsitesolutions.uk is verified (DKIM, SPF and return-path records live in DNS), so mail is signed and lands in the inbox, not spam. Messages are sent from Swift Site Solutions <quotes@swiftsitesolutions.uk>. Every send is recorded in the outbound-message log.
  • WhatsApp — provider Twilio, using the approved business sender +1 555 347 4445 ("Swift Site Solutions", status Online). Used by the supplier RFQ / PO flow. For contacts who haven't opted in, the app falls back to opening WhatsApp with the message pre-filled.

You can confirm both are on at any time from the admin app, or via the status endpoint:

GET /api/admin/messaging/status
{"email":{"configured":true,"provider":"resend"},
 "whatsapp":{"configured":true}}

How to test · 1 of 2

Testing email

Any action in the platform that sends mail is a valid test. The simplest is a portal invite.

  1. Sign in to the Swift platform as a staff / admin user.
  2. Trigger an email action — e.g. invite a client to the portal, send a quote or RFQ by email, or email a statement from the customer portal.
  3. Check the recipient's inbox. The message arrives from quotes@swiftsitesolutions.uk with a verified sender and lands in Focused / Primary, not spam.
  4. Confirm it was logged: it appears in the platform's outbound messages record.

How to test · 2 of 2

Testing WhatsApp

WhatsApp's rule first. A business can only send free-form text to someone within 24 hours of that person messaging the business. To message a brand-new contact, an approved template is required. So a test needs the recipient to say hello first — this is WhatsApp policy, not a limitation of the platform.
  1. From the test phone's WhatsApp, send any message (e.g. "Hi") to +1 555 347 4445. This opens the 24-hour window.
  2. Trigger a WhatsApp send from the platform — a supplier RFQ or PO by WhatsApp to that number.
  3. The message arrives in WhatsApp from Swift Site Solutions, and its status moves to delivered / read.

Evidence

Verified in production

  • Email — real messages delivered to a live inbox (Focused), signed by the verified domain; production status reports email: configured: true.
  • WhatsApp — test message delivered and read on the recipient's phone; production status reports whatsapp: configured: true.
  • Sending path — both run from the deployed backend and are used by the platform's real send flows (portal invites, quotes, RFQs, POs, statements, follow-ups).
